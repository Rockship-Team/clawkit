# Thought Leadership Format

Thought leadership KHAC normal social post. Goal khong phai engagement → goal la **build authority + change conversation in niche**.

## 5 type chinh

### Type A — Contrarian take (opinion against mainstream)

> "Moi nguoi noi X. Du lieu Y noi nguoc lai. Day la 3 ly do toi nghi X la sai."

Pattern:
- Cau dau: framework hien tai duoc cho dung
- Cau 2: counter-data hoac counter-observation
- Body: 3 reason + evidence
- Conclusion: implication action

Vi du seed:
- "Cold email da chet — chua. Day la 3 cohort minh van thay 25% reply rate"
- "Founders khong nen tu sales — sai. Day la 90 ngay minh thu lam sales chinh"

### Type B — Pattern observation (synthesis from many)

> "Trong 6 thang qua, minh noi chuyen voi N founder/SME owner. Day la 3 pattern lap lai."

Pattern:
- Sample size + cohort describe
- 3 pattern observe (specific, khong vague)
- Implication

Vi du seed:
- "30 founder SME minh gap Q2 — 2/3 dau bi cung 1 pain ve sales handoff"
- "20 sales lead chia se workflow — 80% dang tracking pipeline qua Google Sheet"

### Type C — Failure case (admit mistake, share learning)

> "Q1 minh thu X. Fail. Day la ly do va minh hoc duoc gi."

Pattern:
- Hypothesis ban dau
- Action taken
- Result thuc te (with number)
- Why fail
- New hypothesis / new direction

Vi du seed:
- "Em thu cold call 200 SME owner 1 thang — 2% pickup, 0 deal. Day la ly do"
- "Bot daily briefing chay 2 tuan — boss noi 'mo ho khong biet dat duoc gi'"

### Type D — Industry critique (challenge norm)

> "Industry [X] dang lam [Y] sai. Day la chuyen minh nghi nen lam."

Pattern:
- Industry status quo
- Pain customer trong status quo
- Alternative approach
- Caveat (khong phai silver bullet)

Vi du seed:
- "CRM dang ban gia tri sai — minh khong can dashboard, minh can he thong tu nhac toi"
- "AI agent industry dang qua thien performance benchmark — that ra reliable ban giua moi quan trong"

### Type E — Prediction (bet on future)

> "Trong 12-24 thang toi, minh nghi [X] se xay ra. Day la ly do."

Pattern:
- Hien tai
- Force changing (tech / regulation / behavior)
- Prediction specific (avoid generic "AI will change everything")
- Implication action

Vi du seed:
- "12 thang toi, 80% SME B2B Viet Nam se co BD bot. Day la ly do"
- "2 nam toi, vai tro SDR truyen thong se replace 70% bang AI agent"

## Cau truc post chuan (LinkedIn 200-300 words)

```
[Hook 1 line — gioi thieu thought]

[2-3 line setup context]

[Body 3-5 bullet hoac 3-4 paragraph 2-3 cau moi]

[Implication / takeaway 1-2 line]

[CTA — invite discussion, KHONG ban product]
```

## Tan suat

- 1-2 thought post/tuan toi da (not every day — qua nhieu mat weight)
- Sandwich giua educational/discovery post
- Cross-post LinkedIn + Twitter (reformat) + Facebook (Vietnamese audience)

## KHONG lam

- Take contrarian chi de provoke — phai co data backing
- Lap lai pattern hot key cuong dieu ("ngu xua") — sound bad
- Predict generic — phai specific so + timeline
